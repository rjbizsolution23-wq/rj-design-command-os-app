# DELIVERABLE 6 — Complete Logo Usage & Export Specification

## 1. Official Logo Preservation Policy (Non-Negotiable)
- **Official Approved Primary Logo:** `01-LOGOS-AND-MONOGRAMS/rj-official-approved-logo.jpeg` (58,983 bytes, mirrored from official URL `https://storage.googleapis.com/msgsndr/qQnxRHDtyx0uydPd5sRl/media/67eb83c5e519ed689430646b.jpeg`).
- **Rule:** Never alter, stretch, recolor, or silently replace the official logo.
- **Proposed Monogram Extensions (Clearly Marked `PROPOSED-*`):**
  - `PROPOSED-rj-monogram-obsidian-gold.svg` (240×240 vector seal for dark avatars, watermarks, app icons)
  - `PROPOSED-rj-monogram-alabaster-ink.svg` (240×240 vector seal for light stationery & print)
  - `PROPOSED-rj-favicon.svg` (64×64 vector browser favicon)

## 2. Clear Space, Sizing & Usage Rules
- **Clear Space:** Maintain minimum clear space equal to `0.5×` the height of the logo mark on all four sides.
- **Minimum Sizes:** Digital header minimum height `36px` (monogram/favicon minimum `16px`); Print minimum width `1.0 inch` (25mm).
- **Approved Backgrounds:** Sovereign Obsidian (`#0A0B0E`), Charcoal Vault (`#12141A`), Pure Ivory (`#FFFFFF`), Warm Alabaster (`#FAF8F5`).
- **Prohibited Usage:** No drop shadows over busy photos without a dark scrim, no neon outlines, no aspect-ratio distortion, no unapproved color substitutions.
